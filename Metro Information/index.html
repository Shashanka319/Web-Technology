Stations

Major Metro Stations Guide

Explore key stations across all Namma Metro lines with facilities and connecting points.

Yellow Line Stations
R.V. Road

Starting point of Yellow Line. Key interchange with bus terminals nearby.

Facilities: Parking, ATM, Food Court
Bommasandra

Terminal station for Yellow Line. Gateway to Electronic City and IT corridor.

Facilities: Parking, WiFi, Restaurant
Jayanagar

Serves the residential and commercial zone of South Bengaluru.

Facilities: ATM, Medical Store, Shopping
Kengeri

Important interchange point with excellent connectivity to surrounding areas.

Facilities: Parking, Cafe, Information Desk
Green Line Stations
Madavara (BIEC)

Northern terminal of Green Line near Bangalore International Exhibition Centre.

Facilities: Parking, Exhibition Hall Access
Yeshwantpur

Major railway junction with excellent bus connectivity and commercial hubs.

Facilities: Railway Connection, ATM, Food
Yelahanka

Connects residential North Bengaluru with tech parks and educational institutions.

Facilities: ATM, Shopping, Schools/Colleges
Silk Institute

Southern terminus near educational institutions and residential areas.

Facilities: ATM, Information, Parking
Purple Line Stations
Kadugodi (Whitefield)

Eastern terminal serving Whitefield IT hub and surrounding tech parks.

Facilities: Corporate Campus Access, Parking
Whitefield

Major stop in IT hotspot with multiple office parks and commercial establishments.

Facilities: Food Court, ATM, Shopping
Marathahalli

Central business district with IT and tech startups, shopping malls, and restaurants.

Facilities: Shopping Mall, Restaurants, Parking
Majestic

Important interchange connecting Purple and Green lines, central business heart.

Facilities: Bus Terminal, ATM, Information
Pink Line Stations
MG Road

Business and entertainment hub with premium shopping, dining, and corporate offices.

Facilities: Shopping Hub, Restaurants, Nightlife
Cubbon Park

Near iconic Cubbon Park, government buildings, and major commercial establishments.

Facilities: Park Access, Government Offices
Vidhana Soudha

Serves the state legislature and nearby cultural institutions and administrative areas.

Facilities: Government Access, Information
Bannerghatta Road

Southern terminal providing access to residential areas and Bannerghatta National Park.

Facilities: Park Access, ATM, Parking
