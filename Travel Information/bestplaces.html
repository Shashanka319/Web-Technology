Discover Karnataka's Hidden Gems

Karnataka is blessed with diverse landscapes, rich culture, and historic monuments. Here are the best places you shouldn't miss during your visit to this beautiful state.

Mysore Palace

📍 Mysore City

One of the most magnificent royal palaces in India, showcasing stunning Indo-Islamic architecture and intricate craftsmanship.

⏱️ 2-3 hours 💰 ₹900
Popular

Coorg

📍 Kodagu District

The coffee capital of India with aromatic plantations, misty hills, and perfect trekking trails through lush greenery.

⏱️ Multi-day 💰 ₹3000+
Beach

Gokarna Beach

📍 Gokarna Town

A serene coastal destination known for its pristine beaches, laid-back atmosphere, and water sports activities.

⏱️ 2-3 days 💰 ₹2000+
UNESCO

Hampi

📍 Ballari District

UNESCO World Heritage site with ancient ruins of Vijayanagara Empire, stunning temples, and historical architecture.

⏱️ 2-3 days 💰 ₹500

Jog Falls

📍 Shimoga District

One of India's highest waterfalls with breathtaking views, especially during monsoon season with cascading water.

⏱️ 4-5 hours 💰 ₹20

Udupi

📍 Coastal Karnataka

Sacred coastal town famous for Krishna Temple, authentic Udupi cuisine, and beautiful beaches.

⏱️ 2-3 days 💰 ₹1500+
Adventure

Dandeli

📍 Belagavi District

Adventure hub offering white-water rafting, rock climbing, jungle safari, and thrilling outdoor activities.

⏱️ 2-3 days 💰 ₹4000+

Chikmagalur

📍 Coffee Country

Hill station surrounded by coffee plantations, waterfalls, and scenic trekking trails with panoramic views.

⏱️ 2-3 days 💰 ₹2500+
