Budget-Saving Tips

💡 Travel Off-Season

Visit during non-peak seasons (May-June, October) for better rates on accommodations

🍽️ Eat Like Locals

Try street food and local eateries for authentic cuisine at half the cost of restaurants

🚌 Use Public Transport

Karnataka has good public transport network. Save money by using buses and trains

🎟️ Group Discounts

Travel in groups of 10+ to get 10-15% discounts on packages and entry fees

📱 Book Online

Pre-book hotels and activities online for better prices and advance discounts

🎒 Budget Hotels

Consider budget hotels or homestays for comfortable yet affordable stay
