Travel Destinations in Karnataka

Karnataka is a state in southern India known for its beautiful beaches, diverse landscapes, rich culture, and historical sites. Here are some popular travel destinations in Karnataka:

Distance Reference

All distances are measured from Bangalore city center

  1. Mysore Palace

    Distance from Bangalore: 140 km | Best Time: Oct - Feb

    Mysore Palace is a magnificent royal residence located in the city of Mysore. It is known for its stunning architecture, intricate details, and historical significance.

    Famous Tourist Places

    • Brindavan Gardens - Beautiful manicured gardens with fountains
    • Chamundi Hill - Temple with panoramic city views
    • St. Philomena's Cathedral - Gothic architecture church

    Visitor Information

    Entry Fee: ₹300 (Indians) | ₹600 (Foreigners)

    Opening Hours: 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM

    Best Duration: 2-3 hours

    View More Places on TripAdvisor

  2. Hampi

    Distance from Bangalore: 340 km | Best Time: Oct - Feb

    Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with ancient temples, historical ruins, and breathtaking landscapes. It was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire.

    Best Places to Visit

    • Virupaksha Temple - Ancient Hindu temple with intricate carvings
    • Vittala Temple - Famous for its musical pillars
    • Hampi Bazaar - Historic marketplace and trekking paths

    Visitor Information

    Entry Fee: ₹250 (Indians) | ₹500 (Foreigners)

    Opening Hours: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM

    Best Duration: 2-3 days for complete exploration

    View More Places on TripAdvisor

  3. Mangalore

    Distance from Bangalore: 350 km | Best Time: Oct - Mar

    Mangalore is a coastal town in Karnataka known for its beautiful beaches, rich culture, and vibrant local markets. It's an important port city with historical significance.

    Best Places to Visit

    • Panambur Beach - Sandy beach with water sports
    • St. Aloysius Chapel - Historic chapel with intricate paintings
    • Nehru Maidan - Scenic waterfront area

    Visitor Information

    Best For: Beach lovers & Water sports enthusiasts

    Popular Activities: Swimming, Jet skiing, Parasailing

    Local Speciality: Fresh seafood & Coconut-based cuisine

    View More Places on TripAdvisor

  4. Bijapur

    Distance from Bangalore: 470 km | Best Time: Oct - Feb

    Bijapur is a historic city in Karnataka known for its impressive architecture, including the Gol Gumbaz, which is one of the largest domes in the world.

    Best Places to Visit

    • Gol Gumbaz - Majestic dome with acoustic properties
    • Bijapur Fort - Ancient fortification with historical importance
    • Ibrahim Rauza - Beautiful mausoleum architecture
