Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the package?

All packages include accommodation, meals, local transport, and guided tours of attractions. Please check individual package details for specific inclusions.

Can I customize my package?

Yes! Fill our customization form and our team will prepare a tailored package based on your preferences and budget.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancellations up to 30 days before travel date get full refund. Within 30 days, 50% refund is applicable.

Do you provide travel insurance?

Travel insurance can be added to any package at an additional cost for your peace of mind.

What are the payment options?

We accept credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and digital wallets. A 50% advance is required at booking.

Are group discounts available?

Yes! Groups of 10 or more get 10-15% discount. Contact our team for group booking details.
